# Replication presentation clarification

2026-10-03, during the registered replication before its aggregate is available.
The original protocol planned to keep a legacy transfer-vs-default headline and
put the registered primary verdict in a separate section. To give this new study
one unambiguous primary headline, publication instead uses the already registered
**transfer_vs_neutral** contrast and its **0.01** practical threshold for the
study's primary effect, decision rule and aggregate headline. The original
preregistration remains unchanged and its captured hash is retained.

This changes presentation only: the primary contrast, threshold, seed panel,
budget, bootstrap method, source-learning rule and failure policy are unchanged.
The retained per-seed measurements and effect intervals are unchanged. All
secondary comparisons remain published. The old maintained v4 report continues
to declare transfer_vs_disjoint as its original primary effect.

The effects table reports zero-relative confidence verdicts for each comparison;
the registered headline additionally requires practical improvement above 0.01.
Independent confirmation and promotion remain separate, gated work.
